Build me a 3U CubeSat that images the Lake Erie western basin to track harmful algal blooms.

- Fly at 500 km, sun-synchronous, for two years.
- Use two body-mounted solar panels, 0.06 m2 of cells in total, 29 percent efficient.
- Downlink at 1 Mbps on S-band with a 2 W transmitter to our 3 m dish in Akron.
- Keep it under 5 kg at launch.

Parameterize anything another mission might change. Do not parameterize the solar constant.
