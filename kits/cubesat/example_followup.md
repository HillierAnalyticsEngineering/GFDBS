I tested the baseline. Power margin fails, so adopt the deployable panels: 0.12 m2 of cells, and the bus dry mass with hinges is 3450 g.

Our cell vendor qualified the array to 15 years: relative output is 0.70 at 15 years. With that data, a 12-year life should now evaluate. For 0.09 m2, 29 percent cells, pointing factor 0.45, sunlit fraction 0.62 and a 6 W load at 12 years, I expect 7.41 W and a pass.

This program requires 6 dB of link margin.

Apply a 1.2 factor to radiation dose for solar-cycle uncertainty, and make that factor a parameter. With 2 mm, 2 years, 20 krad and a 1.2 factor I expect a mission dose of 9.6 krad and a margin of 2.08.
